# Content and Data Guidance

- Use concrete task language, not promotional claims.
- Page titles identify the place; descriptions clarify its job.
- Include freshness for volatile operational data.
- Include units and time ranges for metrics and charts.
- Prefer short labels and supporting metadata over long explanatory paragraphs.
- Name the responsible service/account/source when multiple exist.
- Use realistic but clearly labelled sample data in demos.
- Do not expose credentials in visible text, screenshots, logs, or examples.
- Keep destructive-action language explicit: identify what will change and whether it can be undone.
